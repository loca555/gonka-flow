WGNK monitor API

Read only. In APP_MODE=mints the old full-mode APIs are disabled. Gonka is queried for source bridge operations, linked addresses and a separate holder balance snapshot.

  • /api/mints?minimum=0 — all stored finalized mints, 50 rows per page.
  • Filters: minimum, hours, q (recipient hex address, hash or request ID), finality=finalized|all.
  • sort: newest, oldest, largest, or time, recipient, amount, tx, status with the _asc or _desc suffix. The whole selection is sorted before limit/offset.
  • /api/mints/tx/{tx_hash} — mints of one transaction.
  • CSV of all mints — up to 50,000 rows, no silent truncation; supports the same filters and sorting.

amount_raw is the exact integer string in nWGNK, amount is a decimal string (9 decimals). ts is Unix UTC; time_local is ISO with an offset, timezone = Asia/Nicosia. Daily aggregates account for daylight saving time.

coverage contains the actual processed intervals, the contract creation block and the final block. Provisional events are not included in totals by default.

Holders

/api/mints/holders?asset=WGNK or asset=GNK. Only addresses with a balance of 10,000 tokens or more, by descending exact balance. q searches part of an address, limit=1..100 (default 25), offset is the offset. rank keeps the position in the full list. sum_raw is the sum of balances of all addresses from 10,000, supply_raw is the total supply; a search does not change these values. share_bps is the share of supply in hundredths of a percent, rounded down.

ready=false and total=null mean there is no verified snapshot, not zero balances. WGNK is restored from the continuous transfer history, including the current verified block. GNK is a paginated census of cosmos.bank.denom_owners at a single block, reconciling the address count, the sum and the ngonka supply. progress shows the course of the first or repeated verification. The last completed snapshot is kept until the next one finishes. The GNK interval is 6 hours. Addresses include contracts and module accounts; GNK means the bank balance, not the total balance of the owner including staking. Private labels are not returned.

Pools, buys and sells

daily contains the total raw (nWGNK), quote_raw (micro-USDT) and separate bought_raw/sold_raw, buy_quote_raw/sale_quote_raw, buys_count/sales_count. Buys + sells equal the total volume of the day. All amounts are exact integer strings, not floats. Aggregates cover the whole filtered history before limit/offset; price_raw is the weighted day price in USDT per 1 WGNK, 12 decimals.

side=all combines buys and sells while keeping separate summary.sold/bought, sale_quote/buy_quote and average prices for each side. kind_asc/kind_desc sorts by trade side. With side=all, summary.volume/quote and average_price are the total gross turnover and the weighted price of all executions, not profit or a net balance.

Minters contain first_mint_ts, last_mint_ts and mint_count: the first/last confirmed mint and the number of mints up to the snapshot block. This data does not depend on trading filters.

Volumes and balances are shown without a fractional part. All prices are USDT per 1 WGNK, with a decimal point and rounded to 6 decimals; a non-zero price below display precision is shown as “< 0.000001”. The API price remains USDT per 1 WGNK with 12-decimal precision. Trading of GNK itself is not tracked in this feed.

/api/mints/flows?side=sell, side=buy — buys. minimum is the minimum volume of one Swap in whole WGNK, from 0 to 1,000,000,000,000 inclusive; default 0. The threshold applies to the whole history before sorting and pagination, as well as to trading totals and the chart. minimum, hours and q are independent of the bridge feed threshold. A full address requires a confirmed WGNK outflow/inflow of the receipt initiator; other hex queries search transaction hashes.

sort: time, actor, amount, quote, price, pool, tx with the _asc or _desc suffix; default time_desc. limit/offset are applied after sorting. Price is compared as an exact ratio of integers.

trades is the selected side of swaps, summary.volume is its turnover. The sales field, kept for compatibility, contains sells only and is empty with side=buy. sold and bought do not mix sides. All trading aggregates include all addresses of the two verified pools, not only mint recipients.

minters is a separate report on addresses that received WGNK from mints and their all-time sales. It does not depend on minimum/side/hours/q. Sales may include tokens bought later: individual lots are not tracked.

ready=false means there is no reconciled snapshot, not zero values. snapshot defines a single final block and the reconciliation of totalSupply / balanceOf with the full history.

Sell price = USDT out / WGNK into the pool; buy price = USDT in / WGNK out. API prices are rounded down to 12 decimals, without gas and possible router fees. Turnover is repeatable, not a share of unique supply. Transfers and liquidity are not buys/sells. Other DEXes, CEX and OTC are not covered.

Address balance and all trading

outside_holders in /api/mints/flows is the distribution of the current balance outside the tracked pools, regardless of minimum/side/hours/q/limit/offset. Investors: bought_raw / (bought_raw + sold_raw) > 90%; sellers: sold_raw / (bought_raw + sold_raw) > 90%; traders: the remaining addresses with confirmed trading, including exactly 90/10. unclassified means no confirmed positive trading volume. Only positive current balances and initiator_net trades of the two pools over the whole final history. The bridge, transfers and LP are not trades. Categories are nominal; an address is not a person.

outside_holders.total_raw is the denominator of ownership shares, the sum of current WGNK outside the two pools. groups contains id, balance_raw/balance, addresses and holders; each holder contains address, balance_raw/balance, bought_raw/bought and sold_raw/sold. Category balances and their lists add up exactly to total_raw, and an address appears in one category. Lists are sorted by descending balance by default. Monetary amounts in *_raw are integer strings, the threshold is checked by exact integer comparison; interface percentages do not affect the category. If the reconciled balance is missing or mismatched, ready=false and total_raw=null — not 0. RPC is not called additionally.

/api/mints/address/{address}?sort=time_desc — a full Ethereum address, all confirmed buys and sells in one response without pagination. Supports the same trading sorts. address_balance contains amount/amount_raw, height, ts and source=verified_transfer_ledger. The balance is computed from all final mint, burn and transfer events up to the snapshot block, regardless of trading filters; the same block limits the trade list. An unconfirmed balance is null, not 0. In /api/mints/flows the balance is also available when q equals a full address.

Fast trade paging

/api/mints/trades returns only table rows and the total, offset, limit, has_more fields. Required: through — snapshot.height from the /api/mints/flows response, and as_of — now from the same response. The minimum, hours, q, side, sort parameters must match the selected feed. The snapshot and the period boundary are kept while paging, even if the indexer has already received new blocks. limit: 1–200, offset: 0–5,000,000. An unavailable or not yet verified snapshot yields ready=false. The request reads only stored trades, does not call RPC and does not recompute the balance distribution, charts or minters.

Bridge feed

/api/mints/bridge?minimum=0 — explicit WGNKMinted and WGNKBurned without the duplicate ERC-20 Transfer. Supports minimum, hours, q, sort (time, kind, recipient, amount, tx, status with _asc/_desc), limit/offset. Only final events up to the reconciled snapshot, without provisional burns. address is the minter or the burn address depending on kind. On the site minimum=0 by default: all volumes are visible. The “Min volume” filter changes minimum only for the bridge feed and its CSV; the summary cards and the mint chart keep the whole history.

For mints, minter_totals contains minted_raw and sold_raw — integer nWGNK strings, minted and sold — exact decimal WGNK strings, sales_count — the number of confirmed sell executions. This is the whole address history up to the snapshot, unaffected by feed filters and pagination. Sales count only initiator_net in the tracked pools and may exceed mints: bought and transferred-in tokens are not separated from minted ones. For burns minter_totals=null.

CSV of the whole feed contains exact amounts and the operation type; a burn on Ethereum does not confirm completion of the reverse transfer to GNK.

Linked Gonka addresses and incoming GNK

/api/mints/provenance, optional address=full Ethereum address. Returns total, verified, pending, sources and links. Links are verified by the final WGNK mint, BLS request ID, epoch, amount, recipient and a successful GNK transfer into escrow in the source block. It does not prove a common owner or GNK mining.

For the reverse direction, burns contains total, verified, pending, items and links: all final burns of the address, including addresses without mints. The completed status and gnk_address appear only on an unambiguous match of the final WGNKBurned with BRIDGE_COMPLETED in the Gonka bridge state by Ethereum block, transaction index, contract and exact amount. evidence_url leads to the verification record. verification=gonka_bridge_state; gnk_tx_hash and gnk_ts remain null — the payout transaction is not established separately. eth_ts is the burn time. Other operations have status=unverified, not a fictitious credit.

sources combines mint senders and recipients of confirmed returns. mints / amount_raw / amount still refer only to GNK → WGNK, burns / burned_raw / burned only to WGNK → GNK. The top-level total / verified / pending / links are kept for mints. Gonka history and balance are also available for addresses linked only to returns. All APIs only read the database.

/api/mints/gonka/{address}?sort=time_desc — all stored incoming GNK in one response, only for bridge-linked addresses. sort: time, amount, sender, kind, tx with _asc/_desc. items: amount_raw (ngonka, 9 decimals), amount, src, source_label, ts, height, tx_hash, event_index, message_index, kind. These are separate events, not aggregated transaction totals. A receipt via CoinReceived does not duplicate Transfer.

history.exhausted is the end of the available GonkaLabs address feed, not independent proof of network completeness. full_chain_verified=false: unindexed internal recipients and automatic payouts in block events may be missing. source and coverage_note state the limits. amount is the sum of loaded receipts, not income, a balance or a proven origin of sold WGNK. Errors keep previously loaded rows and the cursor. The API only reads the cache and does not start an external search.

address_balance is a separate current GNK bank balance: address, amount_raw (ngonka), amount, height from the Cosmos RPC header, checked_at, scope=bank_balance. Funds in separate vesting and collateral modules are not included. The balance is refreshed per address about once a minute and stored in SQLite. On failure the last snapshot remains and balance_status.error reports the error; if there was no successful snapshot, address_balance=null, not zero. The amount sum of incoming transfers is not a balance.

Top buyers and sellers

/api/mints/leaders — all available trading history of the two pools. buyers and sellers contain all ranked addresses: rank (by descending WGNK), address, volume_raw / volume, quote_raw / quote, average_price (USDT per 1 WGNK), swaps, transactions, first_ts, last_ts. summary.buy / sell are the totals of ranked addresses; excluded.buy / sell are the volume, USDT and executions without a confirmed attribution. summary=null when ready=false, not zero turnover. The snapshot, coverage and status fields indicate data freshness and completeness.

price_distribution["5"] and price_distribution["10"] — distributions with a step of 5 and 10 USDT cents. Each has buy and sell lists for the addresses of the respective ranking. from_price_raw is inclusive, to_price_raw is exclusive; the bounds and price_raw (the volume-weighted price of the range) have 12 decimal places. volume_raw / volume are WGNK, quote_raw / quote are USDT, swaps is the number of executions. The price of each Swap is determined by the exact USDT / WGNK ratio, without rounding to the distribution. The amounts and executions of all ranges add up to summary.buy / sell; empty ranges are skipped. When ready=false, price_distribution=null.

price_days["5"] and price_days["10"] — days by price range, shared by buys and sells: total_days is the number of calendar days with ranked trades, bands are the from_price_raw / to_price_raw bounds and the number of days. Each day in the site time zone (Asia/Nicosia) belongs to exactly one range with the largest combined WGNK volume of buys and sells. On equal volumes the lower range is chosen. Days without trades are not added; when the step changes the distribution is recomputed. This is the main range by daily volume, not the exact time the price spent inside it. When ready=false, price_days=null.

The initiator_net criterion matches the address history: the direction of the net WGNK flow of the initiator over the whole transaction. It is not an exact trace of an individual execution, not identification of a person and not profit. Undetermined trades remain in the total trading turnover but are not included in the address ranking. The API reads the existing database and does not start additional indexing.
