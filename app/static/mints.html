LIMITS OF THIS SCREEN

WGNK bridge and market.
Verifiable events.

On Ethereum we track WGNK mints, burns and trades in two verified Uniswap V3 pools. On Gonka — only the source bridge operations and incoming transfers of linked GNK addresses. Full indexing of Gonka history and mining is not run. For the “Holders” tab, a Gonka balance snapshot is separately reconciled with the total GNK supply.

What goes into the bridge feed

WGNKMinted and WGNKBurned events of the official contract. The matching ERC-20 Transfers are not counted twice. A mint is not a purchase; a burn is not a sale and not proof that the transfer to GNK completed.

GNK sources

The link to a mint is verified by the bridge request and the results of the source Gonka transaction. Receipts are taken from the GonkaLabs address feed and stored in the database. The end of the explorer feed does not guarantee complete network history: unindexed recipients and automatic payouts in block events may be missing. A common owner of addresses and a mining origin of coins are not assumed.

Limits of trading data

Only Swaps of two verified WGNK/USDT pools. Trades are attributed to an address when the initiator has a confirmed WGNK inflow or outflow. Transfers, LP, CEX, OTC and other DEXes are not included.

History and precision

Completeness of Ethereum history is checked against processed ranges. Provisional events are excluded. Amounts are shown without a fractional part; positive values below one are shown as “< 1”. Prices are USDT per 1 WGNK with a fractional part. The API and CSV keep full data precision.

Waiting for ranking data…

Ranked by gross execution volume, not by balance or profit. One address can be in both lists. The price is volume-weighted per 1 WGNK, without gas and possible router fees. Click an address to open its buys and sells.

A trade is attributed to an address by the direction of the net WGNK flow over the whole transaction: to the confirmed recipient on a buy or the sender on a sell, and if there is none — to the execution initiator. Complex routes may be resolved incompletely; an address does not mean an established identity. Transfers, the bridge, liquidity, other DEXes, CEX and OTC are not included in the ranking.

Holders

Balance of 10,000+ tokens · by descending balance · a separate list for each network

WGNK tags are based on confirmed trades over the whole history: “Investor” — buys are over 90% of volume, “Seller” — sells are over 90%, “Trader” — neither side exceeds 90%. Exactly 90% counts as a trader. Transfers and the bridge are not included. GNK tags are based on the combined flow of discovered chains through the bridge and subsequent WGNK operations. After mixing, coins are treated as a single balance and executions are counted in full. Volumes of linked flows may overlap and are not personal trade volumes of the source addresses. GNK history is still loading. “Miner” — rewards to the participant are confirmed and strictly less than 10% of those rewards was sold. Exactly 10% does not qualify; transfers and the bridge are not sales.

WGNK is the Ethereum balance including all transfers. GNK is the bank balance on the Gonka network; coins delegated or locked in other modules are not added to the address. The lists also include contracts and module accounts. The share is calculated from the total supply of the respective token.

WGNK is updated together with Ethereum history. The GNK snapshot is verified every 6 hours. An address is not a person; addresses on different networks are not merged.

Preparing local OTC…

By default — any volume. WGNK filters apply separately to trades and the bridge feed. WGNK is the available Ethereum history; GNK receipts are the available explorer address feed. Volumes and balances have no fractional part; prices are USDT per 1 WGNK with a decimal point.
