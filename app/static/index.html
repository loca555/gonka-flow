VERIFIABILITY BEFORE CONCLUSIONS

What exactly Gonka Flow shows

01 / TRADES

Buy and sell

The direction is determined by the sign of WGNK in a Swap of a verified Uniswap V3 pool. Transfers, the bridge and liquidity changes are not trades. Volume is turnover, not an open position.

02 / ADDRESSES

A role, not an identity

The initiator may be an aggregator. The ranking includes initiators with a matching WGNK movement in the receipt. Host labels are based on the current epoch; real owners are not identified.

03 / BRIDGE

Two transfer stages

GNK is locked, WGNK is minted. On the way back WGNK is burned and GNK is released. Without the second side in the index the status means “not matched”, not a proven bridge delay.

04 / REWARDS

Accrual and availability

A successful ClaimRewards does not prove a payout. Monetary events are counted, vesting separately. A regular transfer with vesting is not marked as mining. Weight is taken from active_participants.

05 / COMPLETENESS

History is still loading

The period is a filter, not a coverage guarantee. The archive date and the progress of both networks are shown separately. Ethereum is also restored from the launch of WGNK. The holder census runs independently. Completeness is checked against actual ranges without gaps.

06 / LIMITS

What is not here

CEX/OTC, private settlements, unverified DEXes, internal Cosmos/CosmWasm swaps and other bridges are not covered as trades. Module and pool balances are not distributed among end owners. The historical USD price is not replaced with the current one.

Ethereum before finalized is marked as provisional: this range is rescanned and may change on a reorganization. Public RPCs are trusted sources, not our own cryptographically verifying light client.
